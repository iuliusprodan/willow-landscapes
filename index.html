Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about our work and how we operate.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotations on all driveways, patios and garden projects. Call us or use the online form and we will arrange a site visit.

What areas do you cover?

We work across Staffordshire, Cheshire and Shropshire. If you are nearby and unsure whether we cover your postcode, give us a call and we will let you know.

How long does a driveway take?

Most domestic driveways take between one and two weeks depending on size, access and ground conditions. We will give you a clear timescale when we quote.

Do you handle groundworks and prep?

Yes. We carry out excavation, sub-base preparation, drainage and all groundwork needed before paving or surfacing. Proper prep is part of every job we do.

Can you match existing paving?

In many cases, yes. Bring us photos or samples of your existing paving and we will advise on the closest match and the best approach for extensions or repairs.

Are you fully insured?

Yes. We hold public liability insurance and take care on every site. We treat your home and neighbours with respect throughout the project.
