About Willow Landscapes

Local groundworks specialists with over 15 years of experience creating quality gardens and driveways.

Willow Landscapes & Groundworks Ltd has spent more than 15 years helping homeowners across Staffordshire, Cheshire and Shropshire transform their outdoor spaces. From new driveways and patios to full garden groundwork, we focus on reliable workmanship and clear communication from the first call to completion.

Quality you can see

We take pride in high-quality finishes on block paving, resin, tarmac, porcelain, Indian stone, turfing, fencing and every other aspect of groundworks. No shortcuts, no surprises: just solid results built to last.

Customer-focused

Every project starts with a free quotation. We listen to what you need, explain your options in plain English, and work around your home with respect for your property and neighbours.
